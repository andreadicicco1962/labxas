Prof. Andrea Di Cicco

Born in Pistoia (Italy), 15-Aug-1962
Full Professor in Condensed Matter Physics
Delegate for International Research Networks, UNICAM
Coordinator of Bachelor & Master Physics Degrees (2015-2025)

PhD in Physics (Sapienza University of Rome, 1988–1991). Researcher (1990), Associate Professor (2000), and Full Professor (2018). Author or co-author of over 250 peer-reviewed articles with 10,000+ citations (h-index 51). Recipient of the IXAS Edward Stern Prize (2015) and the SILS Career Recognition Award (2025).
