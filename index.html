Scientific Pillars

Research Activities & Projects

Exploring matter under extreme conditions, advanced battery materials, and synchrotron spectroscopy.

1. Matter Under Extreme Conditions

Synchrotrons & FELs

Investigation of solids, liquids, and amorphous states under extreme megabar pressures and thousands of Kelvins using Diamond Anvil Cells (DACs) and Paris-Edinburgh presses at beamlines including ESRF BM29, SOLEIL ODE, and FERMI Free-Electron Laser.

2. Nanomaterials for Energy Storage

Li-Ion & Beyond

Elucidating the atomic mechanisms of reversible conversion/alloying electrodes and uncovering the fundamental physics of the Solid Electrolyte Interphase (SEI) as an extra-charge reservoir in lithium-ion and solid-state batteries.

3. Advanced Spectroscopy Theory

Methods & RMC

Development of innovative computational modeling combining multiple-scattering EXAFS, Reverse Monte Carlo, molecular dynamics, and X-ray diffraction for non-crystalline materials.

Struttura della Materia

Italiano

Corso fondamentale del 3° anno per la comprensione della struttura atomica, molecolare, quantistica e dello stato solido della materia condensata.

Programma sintetico:

  1. Atomi a un elettrone e idrogenoidi: Equazione di Schrödinger, coordinate sferiche, spin, struttura fine ed iperfine.
  2. Atomi a molti elettroni: Principio di Pauli, approssimazione di campo centrale, accoppiamento LS e jj, tavola periodica.
  3. Interazione radiazione-materia: Regole di selezione, spettri di assorbimento ed emissione, effetto fotoelettrico ed Auger.
  4. Molecole: Approssimazione di Born-Oppenheimer, orbitale $H_2^+$, legame covalente ed ionico, moti vibrazionali e rotazionali.
  5. Reticoli cristallini e diffrazione: Reticolo diretto e reciproco, zone di Brillouin, diffrazione di Bragg-von Laue.
  6. Dinamica reticolare e fononi: Catena mono e bi-atomica, calore specifico (Einstein, Debye).
  7. Gas di elettroni liberi di Fermi: Modello di Sommerfeld, conducibilità elettrica e termica (Wiedemann-Franz).
  8. Bande elettroniche: Teorema di Bloch, modello a elettroni quasi-liberi, isolanti, semiconduttori e metalli.
Testi consigliati: Bransden & Joachain, Kittel, Ashcroft-Mermin
Master Degree in Physics • 6 Credits

Advanced Physics Laboratory

English

Hands-on advanced laboratory course for graduate students covering modern experimental techniques in condensed matter and materials science.

Experimental Modules:

  • Synchrotron Radiation Spectroscopy: EXAFS & XANES data collection protocols and computer analysis.
  • Powder X-ray Diffraction (XRD): Rietveld structure refinement and thermal expansion measurements.
  • Raman & Optical Spectroscopy: MicroRaman vibrational mapping and temperature-dependent phase transitions.
  • Extreme Pressure Techniques: Loading and optical ruby fluorescence calibration in diamond anvil cells.
Location: XAS LAB, Polo di Geologia e Fisica

Highlight • 2017

Is the Solid Electrolyte Interphase an Extra-Charge Reservoir in Li-Ion Batteries?

S. Javad Rezvani, Roberto Gunnella, Agnieszka Witkowska, Franziska Mueller, Marta Pasqualini, Francesco Nobili, Stefano Passerini, and Andrea Di Cicco
ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces, 9 (5), pp 4570–4576 (2017) • DOI: 10.1021/acsami.6b13289

Combining tunable soft X-ray absorption and photoemission to demonstrate that a reversible 5-7 nm layer of alkyl lithium carbonates forms at the SEI surface, fundamentally expanding understanding of battery extra-capacity.
