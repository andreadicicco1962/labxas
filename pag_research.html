Scientific Pillars

Research Activities & Funded Projects

Matter under extreme conditions, advanced battery materials, and synchrotron spectroscopy.

1. Matter Under Extreme Conditions

Synchrotrons & FELs

Investigation of solids, liquids, and amorphous states under extreme megabar pressures and thousands of Kelvins using Diamond Anvil Cells (DACs) and Paris-Edinburgh presses at beamlines including ESRF BM29, SOLEIL ODE, and FERMI Free-Electron Laser.

2. Nanomaterials for Energy Storage

Li-Ion & Beyond

Elucidating the atomic mechanisms of reversible conversion/alloying electrodes and uncovering the fundamental physics of the Solid Electrolyte Interphase (SEI) as an extra-charge reservoir in lithium-ion and solid-state batteries.

3. Advanced Spectroscopy Theory

Methods & RMC

Development of innovative computational modeling combining multiple-scattering EXAFS, Reverse Monte Carlo, molecular dynamics, and X-ray diffraction for non-crystalline materials.
