Transportation Guide

  • ✈️ By Air: Ancona-Falconara "Raffaello Sanzio" Airport (AOI) is 80 km away. Rome Fiumicino (FCO) has direct train connections to Foligno and bus links to Camerino.
  • 🚆 By Train: Nearest railway station is Castelraimondo-Camerino (10 km away on the Civitanova Marche – Fabriano line). Regular Contram bus shuttles connect directly to the campus.
  • 🚗 By Car: From the A14 motorway, exit at Civitanova Marche and follow the SS77 expressway towards Foligno; take the Camerino exit.
