Highest Field Honor • 2015

IXAS Edward Stern Prize

Awarded to Prof. Andrea Di Cicco
🏆

The International X-ray Absorption Society's (IXAS) Edward Stern Prize is the highest recognition for outstanding lifetime achievements in the field of XAFS. Bestowed upon Prof. Andrea Di Cicco during the 16th International Conference on XAFS in Karlsruhe (August 2015) before 550 delegates, honoring seminal contributions to spectroscopy of disordered and extreme matter.

Career Award • September 2025

SILS Career Recognition Award

Italian Synchrotron Radiation Society
🎖️

Awarded by the Società Italiana Luce di Sincrotrone (SILS) in September 2025 in recognition of lifelong distinguished leadership in synchrotron experiments, instrumentation development, and educational mentorship.

Young Scientist Recognition • 2015

Best Poster Award to Dr. Fabio Iesari

Dr. Fabio Iesari, while carrying out his PhD in our laboratory, was awarded the Best Poster Award at the international XAFS 16 conference in Karlsruhe (August 2015) for novel Reverse Monte Carlo modeling of disordered nanomaterials.
