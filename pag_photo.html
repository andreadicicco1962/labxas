Historic Group Milestones

  • 1986: First XAFS Conference attended in Fontevraud.
  • 1995: LURE furnace beamtime experiments.
  • 1997: Founding of the XAS Laboratory website.
  • 2000: High-temperature BM29 station commissioning at ESRF.
  • 2001: Undercooled liquid metal experiments.
  • 2006: Former students and collaborators reunion.
  • 2009: 14th International Conference on XAFS (XAFS 14) in Camerino.
  • 2015: IXAS Edward Stern Prize awarded in Karlsruhe.
  • 2018: XAFS 17 Plenary Lecture in Krakow.
  • 2025: SILS Career Award in recognition of synchrotron achievements.
